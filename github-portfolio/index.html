01 / ABOUT ME

A background in real systems.
A future in software.

My background combines electrical engineering technology, hands-on technical problem solving, and a growing focus on software development. I currently work as a Senior Protection and Control Engineering Specialist at Oncor Electric Delivery, where I test and troubleshoot complex electronics and protection systems that support the electrical grid. My work has involved more than 60 substations and switching stations, assistance with over 20 construction projects, and mentoring two specialists in training.

That experience has shaped the way I approach technical challenges. I value careful testing, understanding how components interact, and finding the underlying cause of a problem. In protection and control work, small details can have significant consequences. I bring that same attention to detail to programming, debugging, and designing systems that behave predictably.

I earned a Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering Technology from the University of North Texas in 2016. I am now pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Game Development at Lamar University, with an expected graduation year of 2027. My coursework and independent projects have given me opportunities to work with C++, C#, Python, Kotlin, Java, Unity, and Android development tools.

My software projects reflect an interest in both interactive experiences and technical systems. I have built a playable Unity maze prototype featuring event-driven game mechanics, developed an autonomous-agent simulation for a vertical farming environment, and worked on an Android application with interactive card-deck features. Each project has helped me improve how I design, implement, test, and explain software.

As I complete my degree, I am interested in opportunities where I can combine practical engineering judgment with software development skills. Gameplay programming, simulation, systems-oriented development, and technical problem solving are areas I want to continue exploring. My goal is to keep building useful software, learn from experienced teams, and contribute with the reliability and professionalism I have developed throughout my engineering career.
